FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Planning your safari? We've got answers.

Find answers to some of the most common questions about travelling through Kenya.

What is the best time to visit Kenya?
Kenya can be visited throughout the year. Different seasons offer different wildlife, landscape and travel experiences.
Can I customize my safari?
Yes. SafariVista journeys can be adapted around your preferred destinations, activities, duration and travel style.
Can I combine safari and beach?
Absolutely. A wildlife safari can be combined with destinations such as Diani Beach and other coastal experiences.
How do I request a booking?
Complete the enquiry form below and provide your preferred destination, dates and travel details.
Do you arrange family safaris?
Yes. Family-focused itineraries can be planned around age, interests, comfort and the type of activities you prefer.
